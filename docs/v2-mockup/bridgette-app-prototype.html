Wine School

The fundamentals

Seven short lessons, each with a worked example and a quick check — taught with this list's own wines.

1 · What the structure words mean

Acidity, body, tannin, sweetness — low / medium / high.

Worked example: Hiedler Löss = high acidity (mouth-watering) + low tannin → bright & food-friendly.

3 · The 6 pairing levers

Fat, salt, acid, sweet, heat, intensity.

Try it: fatty ribeye + high-tannin Claret → tannin binds the fat, both taste smoother.
